# Arithmetic operation words for key_terms extraction in reasoning questions.
#
# Derived from the template vocabulary of:
#   Cobbe et al. (2021) "Training Verifiers to Solve Math Word Problems"
#     (GSM8K, arXiv:2110.14168) — Appendix B template structure.
#   Mirzadeh et al. (2024) "GSM-Symbolic: Understanding the Limitations of
#     Mathematical Reasoning in Large Language Models" (ICLR 2025,
#     arXiv:2410.05229) — Figure 1 and template definitions.
#
# A reasoning question word is included here if it:
#   (a) appears as a core operator or relation word in the GSM template family, OR
#   (b) signals a quantitative change, accumulation, or partition operation
#       in typical arithmetic word problem phrasing.
#
# These words are matched as key_terms so that the informative_word and
# answer_critical perturbation policies target the operationally meaningful
# spans of the question, not merely numeric tokens.
#
# Implements: extract_key_terms_from_reasoning_question in tasks/reasoning.py
